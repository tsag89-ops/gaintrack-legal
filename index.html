04 · FAQ

Questions

Is GainTrack free? +

Yes. GainTrack is free to download and use. A Pro subscription adds AI coaching, unlimited routines and advanced analytics via in-app purchase.

Which platforms does GainTrack support? +

GainTrack is exclusively available on Android via the Google Play Store.

Does GainTrack work without an internet connection? +

Core tracking works on-device. Your data syncs to your account when you're back online.

How do I delete my account or data? +

Use the in-app delete option or email support@gaintrack.app — requests are processed within 30 days. See the Delete Account page.
